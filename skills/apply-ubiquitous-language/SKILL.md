# Skill: apply-ubiquitous-language

**When:** Domain terms added/changed; PM notices synonym drift; after large doc edits; language audit.

## Steps

1. Open `documentation/ubiquitous-language/` (and its README).
2. If glossary empty: do not invent terms; open/confirm GAP-005; propose candidates only with PM confirmation.
3. For each defined term: scan touched docs/tasks for conflicts.
4. Prefer glossary spelling/casing; do not force-translate domain terms into Portuguese if the canonical term is another language.
5. Optionally assume **language-auditor** for a structured pass.
6. Record unresolved items in `GAPS.md`.

## Do not

- Create glossary entries without PM approval.
- Rewrite closed decisions to fit new wording without `record-decision` / supersede.
