# Skill: identify-missing-processes

**When:** After resolving work, friction repeated, or PM says “we keep reinventing this”.

## Steps

1. Capture the recurring situation in one sentence.
2. Check `processes/` and `skills/` for an existing playbook.
3. If missing: draft a **proposal** (skill or process stub) — do not implement a huge framework unasked.
4. Add a `GAPS.md` row or a lightweight process under `processes/` after PM OK.
5. Prefer thin skills over long prose.

## Do not

- Invent company/process facts from other ecosystems (e.g. PayGo playbooks).
